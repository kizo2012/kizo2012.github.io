We do not directly collect, store, or ask for personally identifiable information such as your name, physical address, phone number, or email address within the game. Game progress and local settings are stored locally on your device.

4. Data Retention and Security

We do not maintain external servers storing your personal profile. Any technical or diagnostic data collected by third-party SDKs (such as Unity or Google) is transmitted securely using standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and retained according to those providers' respective retention policies.

TileSum is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

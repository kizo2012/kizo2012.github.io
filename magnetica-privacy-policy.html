1. Advertising and information processed

The Game displays advertisements using Unity LevelPlay, an advertising mediation service. The advertising sources configured for the Game are ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, and Google AdMob (Google advertising through LevelPlay). Unity and Google, together with their advertising partners, may process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and operating system information, app usage and advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The information processed depends on the advertising providers, SDK configuration, permissions, and privacy choices.

This information is used to deliver and measure advertising, limit repeated ads, detect fraud, maintain security, and improve advertising services. Where legally permitted and with consent where required, it may also be used for personalized advertising. Advertising information is shared with Unity and participating advertising providers for these purposes.

See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for Unity’s practices, recipients, international transfers, retention, and privacy choices. This policy covers Unity LevelPlay mediation and the ironSource Ads and Unity Ads sources. For Google AdMob, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google may process advertising identifiers, app set IDs and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device. Google also processes app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic information such as launch time, hangs and energy usage. These are used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These advertising SDK practices do not mean the Game provides a player account or online leaderboard.
